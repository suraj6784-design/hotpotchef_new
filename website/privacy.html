Privacy policy

Working draft under the India DPDP Act, 2023. Same copy as the in-app Privacy screen.

HotPotChef processes account email/phone, delivery addresses, order history, and device push tokens to run the marketplace. We do not sell personal data. Payment cards are handled by Razorpay.

Data is stored on Supabase (currently ap-northeast-1 / Tokyo). A named Data Fiduciary and grievance officer will be published before a public store listing.
