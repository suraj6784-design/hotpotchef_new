FAQs

Do I need an account to browse? Guests can browse Available meals. Checkout requires sign-in.

Why did a dish disappear from search? Feed and search only list Available meals. Archived, closed, paused, cancelled, and sold-out plates are hidden.

When is the chef paid? After the order is marked delivered, not when a catalog meal is paused.
