Cancellation & reschedule

Customers may cancel while an order is awaiting chef approval. After the kitchen accepts, cancellation is at the chef's discretion.

Reschedules are not automatic — message the chef in in-app chat. Refunds for paid Razorpay orders are processed by the operator after a confirmed cancel. Test-mode payments are not live bank credits.
